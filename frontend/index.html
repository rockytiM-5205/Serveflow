How it works

From order to serving, in a few steps

ServeFlow keeps ordering and serving simple, so you can focus on what matters most: great food.

  1. 1. Customers order

    Customers open your link, browse your menu and place an order from their phone. No app to install, no account to make.

  2. 2. Staff manage

    Orders appear on your dashboard within seconds. Confirm them, serve them and record payment, all in one place.

  3. 3. Food reaches customers

    Customers follow their order on their own phone while your team serves it. Payment is taken and recorded at your counter.
